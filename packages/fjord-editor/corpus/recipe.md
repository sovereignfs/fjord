# Flatbread

Makes **six**. Takes about 40 minutes, most of it waiting.

| Ingredient     | Amount  |
| -------------- | ------- |
| Flour          | 300 g   |
| Yoghurt        | 150 g   |
| Baking powder  | 1 tsp   |
| Salt           | ½ tsp   |

1. Mix everything with a fork until it comes together.
2. Knead for 3 minutes. Rest 20.
3. Divide into six, roll thin, and cook in a dry pan, ~2 min a side.

> Tip: a splash of olive oil in the dough makes it softer.
