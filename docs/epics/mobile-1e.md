# Fjord Mobile — 1e — Capture without opening the app

The detail of the tasks in this part of [`clients/mobile/ROADMAP.md`](../../clients/mobile/ROADMAP.md): what each one is, how it is known to be done, and how it was done. The design is [`clients/mobile/DESIGN.md`](../../clients/mobile/DESIGN.md); where it and this file disagree, the design is right. Section numbers (§) refer to it.

## 1e.1 Share sheet

**Status:** 📋 Planned

**Design:** §9

Text and links shared from other apps become a quick note.

**Accepts when:** Sharing from a browser and a messaging app lands a note in `Inbox/`, offline too.

## 1e.2 Home-screen widget

**Status:** 📋 Planned

**Design:** §9

One tap to capture; on iOS WidgetKit, on Android a Glance or AppWidget.

**Accepts when:** Tapping it opens the capture screen ready to type.

## 1e.3 App Intents and Shortcuts

**Status:** 📋 Planned

(iOS) (§9): "New Fjord note" with text, usable from Siri and Shortcuts.

**Accepts when:** A Shortcut creates a note without opening the app.

## 1e.4 Quick-settings tile

**Status:** 📋 Planned

(Android) (§9).

**Accepts when:** Tapping it opens the capture screen.
